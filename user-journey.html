Edge cases
  • Session expired: show the guest view with a quiet “Log in” prompt; keep the cart after login
  • Store closed: the time picker opens straight away and ASAP is disabled Handled in prototype
  • Very large balance (B$1,000+): the store name shortens to fit, never the balance
  • ASAP no longer possible near closing time: ASAP disabled, pick a later slot Handled in prototype
  • Scheduled slot passes while browsing: re-check and offer the next slot at checkout
  • Ingredient sold out at this store: show it greyed out as “Sold out”
  • Half protein without a second one: blocked with “Choose another protein” Handled in prototype
  • Veggie picked with a protein: Veggie replaces it Handled in prototype
  • Price changed since the item was added: show the new price before checkout
  • Item unavailable after a store change: flag it and offer a swap or removal
  • Cart emptied: show the empty state with a way back to the menu
  • No pickup slots left today: say so clearly and suggest another store
  • Chosen slot fills up mid-checkout: offer the next available slot
  • B$ covers the whole total: card payment is $0, so no B$ is earned; say this before paying
  • Voucher invalid, expired or below minimum spend: inline error, other options stay available
  • B$0.00 balance: hide or disable “Use Brandollars”
  • Card declined: stay on the payment sheet; keep the B$ redemption reserved
  • Double tap or slow network: place and charge the order only once
  • Payment succeeds but the page fails to load: the order still appears in Order history
  • B$ earned is pending until pickup: label it as pending, not yet in the balance

Edge cases
  • QR scanned at one store but another store is selected: confirm the store before ordering
  • Old link from the marketing site: still lands on the menu, not an error page
  • “Join Happy Points” card closed: stays hidden for the rest of the visit
  • Store closed: the time picker opens straight away and ASAP is disabled Handled in prototype
  • Scheduled slot passes while browsing: re-check and offer the next slot at checkout
  • Ingredient sold out at this store: show it greyed out as “Sold out”
  • Half protein without a second one: blocked with “Choose another protein” Handled in prototype
  • Logs in from the banner: the cart is kept and the balance appears
  • Logs in with a saved cart from another visit: merge, or ask which cart to keep
  • No pickup slots left today: say so clearly and suggest another store
  • Chosen slot fills up mid-checkout: offer the next available slot
  • Email or phone matches an existing member: suggest logging in to earn B$ on this order
  • No phone given: explain they won’t get a “ready” text
  • Card declined: stay on the payment sheet and keep the details entered
  • Double tap or slow network: place and charge the order only once
  • Wants points after ordering: offer “Sign up to claim B$ for this order”
  • Leaves the page: no order history, so show the order number clearly and email it
